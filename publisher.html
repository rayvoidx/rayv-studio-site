RAYV Studio Publisher

An internal tool, used only by the studio's operator, for publishing our own videos to our own YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos to channels owned by the operator, as private videos.
  • Sets each video's custom thumbnail and scheduled publish time, and adds it to the channel's own playlist.
  • Checks that YouTube finished processing a video before its scheduled time.
  • Shows the operator a private dashboard of view, like and subscriber counts for the same channels.

Who can use it

Only the operator. The tool is not public, not offered to other people, and does not access any channel the operator does not own. Each channel is connected through Google OAuth by the channel owner, and access can be revoked at any time in the Google security settings page.

YouTube API Services used

MethodPurpose
videos.insertUpload our own videos
videos.updateSet scheduled publish time and correct metadata
videos.listCheck processing status and read view and like counts
videos.deleteRemove a defective upload that is being replaced
thumbnails.setSet custom thumbnails
playlistItems.insert / playlistItems.listAdd videos to and list the channel's own uploads and playlists
channels.listIdentify the connected channel and read its subscriber count
