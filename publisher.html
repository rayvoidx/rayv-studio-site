RAYV Studio Publisher

An internal tool, used only by the studio's operator, for publishing our own videos to our own YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos to channels owned by the operator, as private videos that carry the altered or synthetic content disclosure.
  • Sets each video's scheduled publish time and corrects its metadata.
  • Checks that YouTube finished processing a video before its scheduled time, and reads the schedule back.
  • Reads view and like counts of the same channels' own videos, for the operator's private performance review.
  • Updates the operator's own channels' descriptions, keywords and banner images.

Who can use it

Only the operator. The tool is not public, not offered to other people, and does not access any channel the operator does not own. Each channel is connected through Google OAuth by the channel owner, and access can be revoked at any time in the Google security settings page.

YouTube API Services used

MethodPurpose
videos.insertUpload our own videos as private videos
videos.updateSet the scheduled publish time and correct metadata
videos.listCheck processing status, read back the schedule, and read view and like counts
videos.deleteRemove a defective upload that is being replaced (operator-initiated)
playlistItems.listList the channel's own uploads, so the same video is never uploaded twice
channels.listIdentify the connected channel
channels.updateSet our own channels' descriptions and keywords
channelBanners.insertUpload banner images for our own channels
