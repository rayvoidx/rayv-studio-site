Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026 · Applies to RAYV Studio Publisher (the "Tool")

1. YouTube API Services

The Tool uses YouTube API Services. By using the Tool you are also subject to the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

2. Who uses the Tool

The Tool is used only by the studio's operator to manage YouTube channels the operator owns. It has no other users and does not access channels or accounts belonging to anyone else.

3. Information the Tool accesses

With the channel owner's authorization through Google OAuth, the Tool accesses, for the owner's own channels only:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for the connected channel;
  • basic channel information (channel ID, title) and the subscriber count;
  • information about the channel's own videos: video ID, title, publish and scheduled times, privacy status, processing status, duration, and view, like and comment counts.

The Tool does not collect information about viewers, does not read comments' content, and does not access any YouTube account other than the owner's.

4. How the information is used

  • To upload, schedule and manage the owner's own videos.
  • To show the owner a private performance dashboard. Some dashboard figures (for example projections and pace) are calculated independently by the Tool and are labelled as such; they are not provided by YouTube.

5. Storage and retention

  • All data is stored only on the operator's own computer. Nothing is stored on third-party servers.
  • OAuth tokens are kept in files readable only by the operator account and are deleted when access is revoked.
  • View, like and subscriber counts of the owner's channels are kept for performance tracking while the channel remains authorized. Authorization is re-checked at least every 30 days.
  • Video titles and other non-statistical data are refreshed or deleted within 30 days.

6. Sharing

The information is not sold, rented, or shared with anyone, and is not shown to anyone other than the operator. The Tool does not allow third parties to serve content or advertisements.

7. Cookies and device storage

The Tool is not a public website and does not place cookies or similar technologies on any user's device. This informational website does not use cookies or analytics.

8. Revoking access and deleting data

A channel owner can revoke the Tool's access at any time from the Google security settings page. When access is revoked, the Tool's stored tokens and the stored data for that channel are deleted. Deletion can also be requested by contacting us at the address below.
